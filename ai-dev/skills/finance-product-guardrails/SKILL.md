# Finance Product Guardrails

## Overview
Use this skill to keep product and AI behavior positioned as financial research and portfolio decision support. The product should feel premium and ambitious without implying regulated advice, trade execution, or guaranteed outcomes.

## Required Framing
- Say "research," "portfolio intelligence," "risk explanation," "scenario planning," and "decision support."
- Avoid "AI trading bot," "automated advisor," "guaranteed returns," "beat the market," "buy now," and "sell immediately."
- Treat goals as educational planning and habit tracking, not profit guarantees.
- Reward learning, consistency, diversification review, and research discipline.

## Review Checklist
- Does the change avoid direct buy/sell/hold recommendations?
- Does it show assumptions, data freshness, and citations where claims are made?
- Does it include a not-financial-advice boundary near AI, goals, projections, and risk content?
- Does gamification avoid profit-chasing or risk-seeking behavior?
- Does marketing copy sound credible for fintech, banking tech, and wealthtech roles?

## Escalate Or Rewrite
Rewrite any copy or AI instruction that:
- promises outcomes or suitability,
- implies the app executes trades,
- encourages users to take more risk,
- presents AI output as authoritative financial advice,
- hides the limitations of stale or incomplete market data.
