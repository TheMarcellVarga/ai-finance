# Finance Product Guardrails

## Allowed Positioning
- AI financial intelligence workspace.
- Portfolio research and decision-support tool.
- Research room for investors, analysts, advisors, and finance creators.
- Goal planning and educational challenge system.

## Disallowed Positioning
- AI trading bot.
- Robo-advisor.
- Brokerage or execution layer.
- Guaranteed-return system.
- Personalized suitability engine.

## Safe Language
- "Review this risk factor."
- "Compare scenarios."
- "Understand concentration."
- "Research recent filings."
- "Track progress toward a goal."

## Unsafe Language
- "Buy this stock."
- "Sell immediately."
- "Guaranteed to reach your target."
- "This is suitable for your situation."
- "Automated strategy that makes money for you."
