Harbor Components — annual risk report

Annual revenue was $480 million, up from $450 million in the prior year. A single customer represented 24 percent of annual revenue. Three suppliers provided 68 percent of specialized components, and alternative qualification can require six to nine months.

Inventory increased from $72 million to $90 million. Management attributed the increase to longer procurement lead times and buffer stock. The company reported no assurance that higher inventory would prevent future component shortages.
