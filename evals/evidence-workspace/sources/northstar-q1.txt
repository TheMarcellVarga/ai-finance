Northstar Systems — Q1 operating review

Revenue increased from $100 million to $112 million as enterprise renewals improved. Gross margin rose from 58 percent to 61 percent because subscription mix increased. Operating expenses increased 8 percent following security and data-platform hiring.

Management identified customer renewal timing, foreign currency movements, and dependence on two cloud infrastructure vendors as material uncertainties. Cash and equivalents were $84 million at quarter end.
